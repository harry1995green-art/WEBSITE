The Club

Levante UD Sports City

Founded in 1909, Levante UD is one of Valencia's iconic football clubs with a history steeped in Spanish football. Your squad trains at the Levante UD Sports City — the same facilities used by the first team and their elite academy.

The coaching staff are UEFA-licensed professionals who work within the Levante UD methodology, giving players a genuine taste of Spanish professional football culture — positional play, pressing intensity, and technical refinement.

Real Betis Balompié

Real Betis Balompié, founded in 1907, is one of Spain's most beloved clubs and a permanent fixture in La Liga. Based in the passionate city of Seville, the club is famous for its attacking philosophy, fervent fan culture and commitment to developing elite young players.

Your squad trains at the Real Betis training facilities under coaches who live and breathe the Betis way — technical precision, positional intelligence, and that unmistakable Seville flair.

The Club

Caixa Futebol Campus

SL Benfica's Caixa Futebol Campus is the pinnacle of football academy infrastructure — voted the World's Best Football Academy in both 2015 and 2019. The 19-hectare campus houses 9 pitches (including 3 with floodlights), a 86-room on-site hotel, gym, swimming pool, medical centre, and sports science labs.

Your squad experiences the same methodology that has produced world-class players across generations — structured, scientific, and deeply technical. Benfica's coaches are among Europe's most qualified, operating within a system renowned globally.
